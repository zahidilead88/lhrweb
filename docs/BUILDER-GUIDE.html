Using the LHRWEB Website Builder

The builder creates complete, editable websites in about a minute — AI writes the first draft, you make it yours. This guide walks through everything: generating a site, editing sections, the AI tools (rewrite, regenerate, SEO, theme), the Figma-style free canvas, publishing, and exporting real code.

1 — Creating a Website

Log in with a builder account and open /builder. If you don't have a project yet (or click New Website ↺ in the editor's top bar), you'll see the choice screen:

🪄 Build with AI
A 4-step wizard: business name → business type → short description → theme. The AI plans your pages, then writes every section with real copy about your business. Best way to start.
✋ Build Manually
Enter a business name, tagline and brand color. You get a blank Home page and add every section yourself from the section library.

2 — While It Generates

Generation runs in two phases and usually takes 30–60 seconds:

1
Planning — the AI decides your pages and sections, picks a brand color and writes a tagline. You see a skeleton preview during this phase.
2
Building pages — each page appears as a live progress chip: Queued → Building… → ✓ Done. Two pages build at a time.
  • If one page fails, the rest of your site still arrives — the failed page is created empty so you can fill or regenerate it.
  • If the AI service is completely down, you get a clean starter template (with your business name) instead of an error — everything in it is editable.
  • Closing the tab mid-generation is safe: pages finished so far are saved, and reopening the builder offers Retry generation / Start fresh.

4 — Working with Sections (Flow Mode)

In Flow mode your page is a vertical stack of sections (hero, services, testimonials, pricing, FAQ…). Hover any section for its action buttons; click it to open the editor panel.

  • Reorder — drag a section by its grip handle, or use ↑/↓ with it selected
  • Duplicate — Ctrl + D · Delete — Delete (the last section on a page can't be deleted)
  • Add sections — Ctrl + A opens the library; or the AI tab: type "a pricing section with 3 plans and a FAQ below it" and it appears with real content
  • Regenerate — the ✨ action redesigns a section; see AI Features for the versions bar

5 — The Section Editor Panel

Click a section and the panel slides in from the right with three tabs:

Content
Every text field of the section — headline, subheadline, buttons, and repeating items (service cards, FAQ entries, pricing tiers) you can add, edit, reorder and remove. Filled text fields show a ✨ Rewrite button (below).
Style
Backgrounds (solid or gradient), heading & body colors, accent color, card backgrounds, padding, heading size, button shape (rounded/pill) and variant (filled/outline), plus layout options like grid columns for card sections.
Advanced
Extra fields and per-section fine-tuning that doesn't fit the two tabs above.

Changes apply live on the canvas. Nothing is permanent until you hit Publish — and Ctrl + Z undoes anything.

6 — Pages & SEO

Use the pages control in the top bar to switch, add, or rename pages. Each page has its own sections and its own SEO settings.

Per-page SEO
1
Open the page → click SEO in the top bar.
2
Click Generate with AI — it reads your page's actual headings and text and writes a search-optimized title (≤60 chars), meta description (~155 chars) and keywords. Character counters turn red past the limits.
3
Edit anything you like, then Save SEO.

7 — AI Features

✨ Regenerate a section — with versions

Regenerating never destroys your work. A dark bar appears at the bottom of the screen:

Version 2 of 3  ◀ ▶  ·  ✨ Try another  ·  Apply  ·  Discard
  • Version 1 is always your original — cycle back to compare, Discard restores it exactly
  • ✨ Try another generates a different take (up to 3 new versions per session)
  • Apply keeps whichever version is on screen
✨ Rewrite any text — in 5 tones

In the section editor's Content tab, click ✨ Rewrite next to any filled text field. Pick a tone — professional · friendly · confident · playful · luxury — and choose one of 3 alternatives. Only that field changes; your design stays put. The builder learns: pick the same tone a few times and future AI copy leans that way automatically.

🎨 AI Theme

Click Theme in the top bar and describe a look — "warm and earthy, like a specialty coffee brand". You get a full palette (7 swatches) and a font pairing as a preview — nothing is applied yet. Apply to adopt it (new sections use it immediately) or Discard for zero trace.

🤖 AI section generation

The AI tab in the sidebar builds new sections from a plain-language request. It already knows your business, brand color and existing style, so results match your site. Works in both Flow and Free canvas modes.

🧠 What the AI remembers

The builder keeps a small, fully visible memory per project: a summary of your business, your preferred tone, and preferences you set. It's what makes the 10th section you generate match the 1st. You can view, edit or wipe it any time (Settings → AI, or via the API) — wiping resets the AI to first-run behavior.

9 — The Free Canvas (Figma Mode)

Toggle Flow → Free in the top bar for an infinite, Figma-style canvas: design in frames, place elements anywhere, then convert frames back into page sections.

Navigation
  • Pan — hold Space and drag, middle-mouse drag, or the Hand tool (H)
  • Zoom — Ctrl + scroll · Ctrl + 0 fit everything · Ctrl + 1 100% · Ctrl +/−
  • Grid — press G for a dot grid · rulers run along the top and left
Frames (artboards)

Press F and drag to draw a frame, or pick a preset: Desktop 1440×900 · Laptop 1280×800 · Tablet 768×1024 · Mobile 390×844. Frames hold your design; the Convert action turns a finished frame into a real page section (HTML + CSS) you can use in Flow mode.

11 — Selecting & Arranging

Selection
  • Click selects (purple outline + a label chip showing the element's tag/name) · Shift+click adds to selection · Ctrl + A selects all
  • Rubber-band — drag on empty canvas; only elements fully inside the box get selected
  • Tab / Shift + Tab — step through elements one by one
Moving & transforming
  • Drag to move — purple snap guides appear when edges/centers align with other elements
  • Arrow keys nudge 1px, Shift+arrows 10px · corner/edge handles resize · the top handle rotates
  • Alt + drag duplicates — the copy moves with you, the original stays
Organize
  • Lock Ctrl + Shift + L — element shows a hatch pattern and can't be moved or selected by drag
  • Hide Ctrl + Shift + H — removed from view (bring back via Layers or the context menu)
  • Z-order — ]/[ forward/backward, Shift + ]/Shift + [ to front/back
  • Copy/paste — Ctrl + C/V (pastes with a 10px offset), Ctrl + Shift + V pastes in place, Ctrl + D duplicates
  • Right-click any element for the full menu: copy, paste, paste in place, duplicate, lock, hide, z-order, delete
  • Align & distribute — select 2+ elements for the alignment toolbar
💡
You can paste designs directly from Figma — copy a frame in Figma and Ctrl + V on the canvas. The builder converts it into editable elements.

12 — Save, Preview & Publish

  • Publish (or Ctrl + S) saves the current page live — the button shows Saving… → Saved ✓. If a save fails it turns red with a Retry; your work stays in the editor either way.
  • Preview opens the page in a new tab at real size, no editor chrome.
  • Your live site — every project gets a URL from its name (slug); you can also view it at /site/<project-id>.
  • Custom domain — connect your own domain to the project; verification is a one-time DNS check.
⚠
Publish saves the page you're on. Edited three pages? Save each one (switching pages prompts you if there are unsaved changes).

15 — Troubleshooting

You see…What it means / what to do
"Monthly AI quota exceeded"You've used this month's AI units. Manual editing still works fully; quota resets on the 1st, or upgrade your plan.
"Too many AI generation requests"Burst limit (5/min). Wait a few seconds and try again.
"AI is temporarily paused / unavailable"The AI provider is having issues; the builder backs off automatically and recovers itself within minutes. Your content is untouched.
"AI was unavailable, so we started you from a template"Site generation fell back to a starter template. Everything is editable — regenerate sections later when AI is back.
"Generation seems stuck"A previous generation didn't finish. Choose Retry generation or Start fresh.
"Review this section" toast after AIThe AI result passed validation but scored lower on quality checks — give it a look, or hit ✨ Try another.
Red Retry on the Publish buttonThe save didn't reach the server (connection blip). Your work is still in the editor — click Retry.
Variant bar says "limit reached"3 generated versions per session. Apply or Discard, then regenerate again if you want more.
